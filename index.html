Welcome to the "Collection of Stories," a bespoke digital archive curated by the technology and design panels of STI College Las Piñas.

This digital project shifts traditional gallery website patterns. Instead of viewing isolated frame assets, the interface organizes the paintings as structured components of an interactive journal desk. These frames suspended in the 3D portal contain the exact visual and mathematical rules designed by the master artists.

From the psychological distortion vectors of Francis Bacon to the divine grid geometries of Leonardo Da Vinci, each room explores key landmarks of artistic storytelling. Hovering over any frame reveals the underlying structural blueprint, allowing visitors to examine compositions through a classical scholar's perspective.
